Bundled typeface: PB Latin Modern Roman

These four files are a modified version of Latin Modern Roman 10 (version 2.004),
from the GUST e-foundry, as distributed on CTAN:
  https://ctan.org/pkg/lm   (fonts/lm/fonts/opentype/public/lm/lmroman10-*.otf)

Changes from the original:
  - subset to Latin (Basic Latin, Latin-1, common punctuation and arrows)
  - converted from OpenType (.otf) to WOFF2
  - renamed to "PB Latin Modern Roman" (files pb-lmroman-*.latin.woff2), as the
    GUST Font License requests for derived works
